Primary Feeder Conductors — NEC 310.16
Primary line (phase) conductors. Defaults to 2 for single-phase, 3 for three-phase -- a primary feed is typically a 3-wire delta run with no neutral. Excludes the equipment grounding conductor, added separately below.
Secondary Feeder Conductors — NEC 310.16
Physical conductors in the raceway: secondary line (phase) conductors plus a neutral -- assumed always present on a transformer secondary feeder. Defaults to the line count (2 single-phase / 3 three-phase) plus 1, and stays in sync with phase until edited directly. Excludes the bonding jumper, added separately below.
NEC doesn't numerically define “major portion of the load” — the common engineering convention (not codified in the NEC) is roughly >50% nonlinear, so this is an engineering judgment call, not a hard threshold. A transformer secondary commonly feeds panels with nonlinear loads (computer rooms, VFDs, LED/electronic ballasts), so the neutral can end up current-carrying.
