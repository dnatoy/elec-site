Conductor and Raceway Sizing

Live sizing per NEC Table 310.16, Table 310.15(B)(1), Table 310.15(C)(1), and Annex C conductor fill tables.

Circuit Inputs

Ampacity Basis — NEC 310.16
Conductors are sized directly to this rating (standard 240.6(A) sizes only). Also drives the Table 250.122 equipment grounding conductor size and the schedule notation below.
Temperature rating of the lowest-rated termination in the circuit (NEC 110.14(C)). This sets the termination limit only — ampacity correction and adjustment always apply to the 90°C column (NEC 310.14(A)(1)), since MDP installs 90°C-rated wire. Suggested as 60°C for OCPD ≤60 A unless you choose otherwise.
Select the rating based on the lowest-rated termination in the circuit (NEC 110.14(C)). Standard ambient is 30°C (86°F). Suggested as 60°C for OCPD ≤60 A unless you choose otherwise.
Suggested as aluminum for OCPD ≥100 A unless you choose otherwise.
Current-Carrying Count — NEC 310.15(E)
Physical conductors in the raceway: line conductors plus a neutral if present. Excludes the equipment grounding conductor, added separately below. Defaults to 4.
Determines whether the neutral is excluded from the Table 310.15(C)(1) current-carrying count per 310.15(E)(1). Doesn't affect the raceway fill count above.
NEC doesn't numerically define “major portion of the load” — the common engineering convention (not codified in the NEC) is roughly >50% nonlinear, so this is an engineering judgment call, not a hard threshold. Only applies to 4-wire, 3-phase wye circuits; a single-phase 120/240V neutral's status is governed separately by 310.15(E)(1) regardless of load type.
Raceway & Fill
Adds 1 conductor to fill only — grounding conductors aren't counted for ampacity adjustment (310.15(F)).
Caps the size used before paralleling.
